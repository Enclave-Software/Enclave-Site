why does this exist

i live in manipur. internet shutdowns by the government are real here (even in the age of Ai slop). when they happen, apps like whatsapp and upi just stop working because they need central servers that are suddenly unreachable. that's a really stupid but easy design. i wanted to try building something different, something great.

the goal is to reduce how much stuff depends on one server or one company staying online. communicate, organise, publish, host - but in a way where if one thing goes down, the rest can keep going.

this is a very big project that i plan to keep trying over and over again if it doesn't work because my stupid brain decided to dream of a project as big as this.


so i will give this my all.

ill complete it even if it takes me more than like 10 years i guess... big Ai tech corps can go to hell.

the ecosystem

// project enclave is not just one piece. there are a few planned pieces

very very early alpha

enclave messenger

messaging. this is the current one. works a bit already, some people are testing it. not public yet.

by some, i mean like 3 people. i don't really have that many tech friends :(

next

stream

site hosting. the idea is you can host a small site inside the enclave network. heavily inspired by tor-style distribution.

future

node

a blog platform thing. kind of like a blog. for publishing without needing medium or substack.

future

sphere

a browser for viewing enclave sites and nodes. it's the last piece that ties everything together i think.
